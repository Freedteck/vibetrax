## **Artist Upload & Revenue Split (1:25 - 2:30)**

**[Screen: Connect artist wallet, navigate to Upload page]**

**Voiceover:**
"The artist connects their wallet and navigates to Upload.

Fill in track details: title, description, genre.

Upload the audio file and album artwork — both stored on IPFS via Pinata.

Set the base price — 0.5 MOVE — and royalty percentage for future resales."

**[Screen: Scroll to collaborators section]**

"Now the critical part: adding collaborators.

Add the producer's wallet address, role 'Producer', and assign a 30% revenue split.

Add the writer with role 'Songwriter' and 20% split.

The artist keeps the remaining 50%.

The platform validates that splits total 100%. Everything is transparent and enforced by the smart contract."

**[Screen: Click Upload, sign transaction]**

"Sign the transaction. Movement processes it in under 2 seconds.

The music NFT is minted with all collaborator details embedded on-chain."
